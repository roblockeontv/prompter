Controls probe

Pair or plug in the controller, then press a button on it once so the browser can see it. Move every stick, wheel and pedal through its whole travel and press every button. Then Copy report and paste it.

For a stick or wheel that varies the speed: press Clear, hold it just off its rest for five seconds, let go; then hold it at full travel for five seconds, let go; then Copy report. The gaps between presses will show whether the travel is carried in how fast it repeats.
